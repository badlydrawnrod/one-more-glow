Controls help

Keyboard Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move, Enter or Space to activate, and Escape to close a detail or exit fullscreen.

Mouse and touch Drag the calibration handle or enter a width. Scroll and pinch zoom outside the handle.

Standard gamepad Move with the D-pad or left stick. Press A to activate or edit, B to go back, and LB/RB to switch screens. In numeric edits, use left/right for small steps and up/down for large steps.

Connect a standard-mapped gamepad to navigate.
